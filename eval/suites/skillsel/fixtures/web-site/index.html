Why we built it

We kept missing the tide. Paper tables are printed months ahead, apps bury the one number you need under ads, and the harbour office closes at five. Tidewater shows the next high and low water for your harbour, the height, and whether your boat clears the sill, on one screen, offline, the moment you open it. We sail out of Lymington and test every release on our own boat before it ships.
